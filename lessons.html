Lesson 1: Parts of Speech

▶️ Watch Video on YouTube

Objective: Identify and use the 8 parts of speech correctly.

Notes: Every word in English belongs to a group.

  • Noun: Person, place, thing. Example: Taiwo, FUOYE, Book
  • Verb: Action word. Example: Run, Teach, Write
  • Adjective: Describes noun. Example: Beautiful girl
  • Adverb: Describes verb. Example: Runs quickly
