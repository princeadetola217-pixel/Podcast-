Effects of Podcasts-Based Instructional Strategy on Students' Learning Outcome in English Language

Project Information

Researcher: ADEBISI TAIWO LATIFAT

Supervisor: DR. ADEGBOYEGA, S.M.

Department: Educational Technology, FUOYE

Course Code: CSC 311

Academic Session: 2024/2025

Target: 300L Students

1. Background

Many 300L students struggle with English listening and speaking skills. This project uses Podcasts to provide flexible, repeatable audio learning to improve learning outcomes.

2. Objectives

  • Develop podcast-based platform for 300L English
  • Determine effect on listening and speaking skills
  • Examine if podcast improves learning outcomes

3. Key Features

  • 🎙️ 8 Podcast Lessons with Audio + Recording
  • 📖 8 Detailed Lessons with Video + Image + Notes
  • 📋 20-Question Timed Post-Test with Certificate
  • 📊 Progress Tracking Dashboard
  • 📱 Fully Responsive for Phone and Laptop
